KikiVoice

Upload a 3–15 second sample, type your text, and download speech in the cloned voice — usually within about 3 minutes. It offers three models (Kiki Core for speed, Kiki Pro for emotion, Kiki Multilingual for language work) and claims 75+ supported languages. Free credits reset every week.

The open-source cloning stack

Six real models from GitHub, verified for licence, language support and hardware (checked 2026-10-26). These run on your own machine or a rented GPU — that is the price of “free forever”: no per-character bills, but you provide the computer. Filter by what matters to you. Only two of the six support Hindi — Chatterbox and XTTS-v2; the other four do not.

Licence caution: a code licence does not by itself prove commercial rights for model weights. Code and weights can carry different licences — every card below lists them separately, and the weights licence is the one that decides commercial use.

MIT · CODE + WEIGHTSHINDI ✓ NATIVE

Chatterbox

Resemble AI — the top recommendation of this stack

Zero-shot cloning from roughly 5–10 seconds of reference audio, with emotion-intensity control and paralinguistic tags like [laugh] and [sigh]. In Resemble AI's blind listening tests it was preferred over ElevenLabs — and the MIT licence covers the weights too, so commercial use is genuinely allowed. Multilingual build covers 23 languages, Hindi included.

  • Sample~5–10 seconds
  • HindiYes — native
  • UrduNo
  • HardwareGPU 8–10 GB · Nano variant runs on CPU
  • Commercial useYes — MIT
  • LicenceMIT — code + weights
  • WatermarkInaudible PerTh watermark on outputs

By design, every Chatterbox output carries an inaudible PerTh watermark — you can't hear it, but the audio can be identified as AI-generated. Treat that as a safety feature, not a flaw.

GitHub — resemble-ai/chatterbox
MITCROSS-LINGUAL

OpenVoice

MyShell AI (with MIT researchers)

Instant cloning with strong style control — emotion, accent, rhythm, pauses. Its party trick is cross-lingual cloning: record in one language and speak in another the model was trained on, even if that pairing never appeared in training data.

  • SampleA few seconds
  • LanguagesEnglish, Spanish, French, Chinese, Japanese, Korean
  • HindiNo
  • UrduNo
  • HardwareGPU ~4 GB
  • Commercial useYes — MIT
  • LicenceMIT

Best pick for English / Spanish / French / Chinese / Japanese / Korean work where a light GPU footprint matters. Hindi is not supported.

GitHub — myshell-ai/OpenVoice

RVC-Boss

Two gears: zero-shot cloning from a 5-second clip, or a quick fine-tune on about 1 minute of audio for the closest similarity of any model here. Ships with a full WebUI and tools for slicing, transcribing and labelling your training audio.

  • Sample5 sec zero-shot · 1 min fine-tune
  • LanguagesChinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Cantonese
  • HindiNo
  • UrduNo
  • HardwareCPU inference OK · GPU speeds training
  • Commercial useYes — MIT
  • LicenceMIT

Choose this when one voice matters most and you can give it a full minute of clean recordings — the fine-tune is where its quality jumps.

F5-TTS

SWivid

Flow-matching architecture that produces some of the smoothest, most emotional cloned speech in open source, from just a few seconds of reference. It supports English and Chinese only — Hindi is not supported. The catch is in the licence split below — read it before building anything you plan to sell.

  • SampleA few seconds
  • LanguagesEnglish, Chinese
  • HindiNo
  • UrduNo
  • HardwareGPU recommended
  • Commercial useNo — weights are non-commercial
  • LicenceCode MIT · weights CC BY-NC

Licence trap: the F5-TTS code is MIT, but the released model weights are CC BY-NC. Personal and research use is fine; monetised products are not covered.

The honest Urdu panel — اردو کلوننگ کی اصل صورتحال

سچی بات یہ ہے: 26 اکتوبر 2026 کو جانچے گئے ماڈلز میں سے کسی بھی چھ ماڈل میں اردو کلوننگ نہیں ہے — Chatterbox، OpenVoice، GPT-SoVITS، CosyVoice، F5-TTS اور XTTS-v2، کسی میں بھی نہیں۔ جو بھی ویب سائٹ آپ سے اردو کلوننگ کا وعدہ کرے، پہلے اس سے ثبوت مانگیں۔

اردو میں جو چیز واقعی دستیاب ہے وہ ہے تیار شدہ TTS آواز: Piper کی اردو آواز «فصیح» (ur_PK) — یہ آپ کی آواز کی نقل نہیں، ایک بنی بنائی اردو آواز ہے جو عام کمپیوٹر پر بھی چلتی ہے۔

اچھی خبر یہ ہے کہ ہندی کلوننگ موجود ہے — مگر صرف دو ماڈلز میں: Chatterbox (MIT لائسنس، تجارتی استعمال جائز) اور XTTS-v2 (وزن غیر تجارتی لائسنس کے تحت)۔ باقی چار ماڈلز ہندی سپورٹ نہیں کرتے۔

The plain truth: in the models checked on 2026-10-26, none of the six models supports Urdu cloning — not Chatterbox, OpenVoice, GPT-SoVITS, CosyVoice, F5-TTS or XTTS-v2. Urdu exists only as the Piper ur_PK “Fasih” preset TTS voice, which is not cloning. Any site promising Urdu cloning should be asked for proof.

What Urdu does have is a ready-made TTS voice: Piper's ur_PK voice “Fasih”. It speaks Urdu text aloud in a fixed, built-in voice — useful, CPU-friendly, and genuinely free — but it is not a clone of you or anyone else.

Hindi cloning is real and available — Chatterbox supports Hindi natively under MIT. For Urdu-script work today, the honest options are: ready-made Urdu TTS (Piper “Fasih”), Hindi cloning (Chatterbox), or a paid cloud service.

This page will never promise Urdu cloning. When a real open Urdu cloning model appears, this panel is the first thing that gets updated.

Piper

Rhasspy / Open Home Foundation

A fast, local text-to-speech engine with 35+ languages that runs happily on plain CPU — even a Raspberry Pi. Its voice catalogue is the reason it's here: it includes the Urdu ur_PK “Fasih” voice and Hindi voices, fully offline.

  • UrduYes — “Fasih” (ur_PK), ready-made
  • HindiYes — ready-made voices

How it works — the command flow

Whichever route you pick, the workflow is the same five steps. Give the command; the model does the work.

STEP 1

Record or upload

3–15 seconds of clean speech — quiet room, no music, one voice. Use the console above; it never leaves your device.

STEP 2

Type the text

Write or paste the words the voice should say. Punctuation shapes the delivery — commas breathe, exclamation marks lift.

STEP 3

Pick the model

KikiVoice for instant free results in the browser, or an open-source model on your own GPU for unlimited, private runs.

STEP 4

Preview

Listen before you commit. Too flat? Re-record a livelier sample — sample quality decides clone quality more than any setting.

STEP 5

Export

Download WAV for editing or MP3 for publishing, and drop it straight into your video timeline.

Consent firstYour voice, or a voice with clear permission. Nothing else — ever.

Secure handlingPrefer local, open-source runs for private voices — your sample never leaves your machine.

Auto-deleteKikiVoice deletes uploads after processing; locally, delete samples you no longer need.

No impersonationNever clone celebrities or anyone else to make them “say” things they didn't say.
